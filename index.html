    Why it's simple

    If you can run a meeting, you can run an AI team.

    • Keep your agents

      No new agents to buy or train. Use the ones you have.

    • Your existing Meet

      The team joins the Google Meet you already use.

    • Just talk

      Say it once, out loud. Every agent hears it. No pasting context.

    You stay in charge. When an agent needs a yes from you, it raises its hand and asks.

    Use cases

    What you can hand off.

    1. Launch a campaign

      Your writer, builder and analyst agents share one ask: emails, a banner, a budget check.

    2. Fix and tell

      Your coding agent fixes it. Your writing agent tells customers.

    3. Research fast

      Your research agent digs in. The others hear the results at once.

    4. Run the morning check-in

      Each of your agents says what it did, what's next and what it needs.
