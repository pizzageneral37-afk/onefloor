What it is

One call. The agents you already use, working as a team.

We don't make agents. You bring yours. Your AI Team puts them on the call with you, in any meeting app, and gives each a name, a role and a voice. They all hear the same conversation, so they share the context. You see who is doing what. When one needs your OK, it raises its hand.

How it works

Three steps. That's it.

    Connect your agents

  3. 3

    Say what you need

    Why it's simple

    • Keep your agents

      No new agents to buy or train. Use the ones you have.

    • Any meeting app

      The team joins the call you're already on, in any meeting app.

    • Just talk
